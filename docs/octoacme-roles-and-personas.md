# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Managers

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers support delivery health by guiding engineering execution, team capacity, technical planning, and developer growth. They help ensure that implementation decisions are sustainable and aligned with project goals.

### Responsibilities
- Assess team capacity and help plan realistic delivery commitments
- Coordinate technical planning, staffing, and engineering dependencies
- Coach developers and support effective collaboration
- Surface technical risks, trade-offs, and resourcing concerns
- Promote engineering quality, maintainability, and observability

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partner with the Project Manager on capacity, dependencies, delivery risks, and execution forecasts
- Partner with the Product Manager to clarify feasibility, sequencing, and technical trade-offs
- Support Developers with technical direction and remove organizational impediments without replacing their implementation ownership
- Escalate unresolved delivery or resource constraints through the Project Manager and appropriate leadership

---

## QA Leads / Test Managers

### Role Summary
QA Leads or Test Managers define the quality approach for a project and help the team verify that deliverables meet acceptance criteria and release standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define the test strategy, coverage expectations, and quality gates
- Coordinate unit, integration, end-to-end, exploratory, and manual testing as appropriate
- Review acceptance criteria for testability and completeness
- Track defects, quality risks, and readiness for milestones or releases
- Ensure test evidence and known issues are documented

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with the Product Manager to confirm acceptance criteria and expected user outcomes
- Work with Developers to plan test coverage, reproduce defects, and improve automation
- Work with the Project Manager to report quality status, risks, and release readiness
- Advise Stakeholders and Sponsors when quality risks affect scope, timing, or launch decisions

---

## Security / Compliance Leads

### Role Summary
Security or Compliance Leads identify security, privacy, regulatory, and policy requirements and help the project address them before release.

### Responsibilities
- Identify applicable security, privacy, and compliance requirements
- Review designs, data flows, and implementation plans for risk exposure
- Coordinate security assessments, threat modeling, and required approvals
- Track findings, mitigations, exceptions, and residual risk
- Define escalation requirements for security or compliance incidents

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partner with Developers and Engineering Managers on secure design, implementation controls, and remediation
- Partner with the Product Manager to balance customer outcomes with security and compliance obligations
- Partner with the Project Manager to record risks, dependencies, decisions, and approval milestones
- Escalate material risks to Stakeholders or Sponsors and coordinate with the security incident process when needed

---

## Data / Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data or Analytics Leads connect project work to measurable outcomes by defining instrumentation, reporting, and analysis needed to evaluate success.

### Responsibilities
- Translate project goals into measurable product and operational metrics
- Define instrumentation, data quality checks, dashboards, and reporting needs
- Establish baselines and monitor adoption, usage, performance, and impact
- Identify trends and communicate evidence-based recommendations
- Document metric definitions and assumptions so results are interpreted consistently

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with the Product Manager to define success metrics and evaluate customer or business impact
- Work with Developers and QA Leads to ensure required events, data, and validation are implemented
- Work with the Project Manager to include measurement activities and reporting milestones in the plan
- Provide Stakeholders and Sponsors with outcome reporting to support decisions and prioritization

---

## Customer Success / Support Partners

### Role Summary
Customer Success or Support Partners represent operational and customer-facing perspectives throughout delivery, rollout, and post-release adoption.

### Responsibilities
- Bring customer needs, feedback, support trends, and operational constraints into planning
- Identify affected users, enablement needs, and rollout or migration concerns
- Prepare support documentation, training, and escalation guidance
- Monitor feedback and issues after launch and coordinate follow-up
- Help validate whether delivered changes solve customer problems in practice

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partner with the Product Manager to connect customer feedback to priorities and acceptance criteria
- Partner with the Project Manager on communications, rollout planning, readiness, and issue escalation
- Work with Developers and QA Leads to reproduce customer issues and verify fixes
- Provide Stakeholders and Sponsors with adoption signals, support impact, and customer risks

---

## Stakeholder / Executive Sponsors

### Role Summary
Stakeholder or Executive Sponsors provide strategic direction, organizational support, and timely decisions when projects require authority beyond the delivery team.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm strategic alignment, priority, and intended business outcomes
- Approve significant scope, funding, timeline, or risk decisions
- Resolve cross-functional conflicts and remove organizational barriers
- Champion the project with affected teams and stakeholders
- Review status, escalation decisions, and outcome measures at agreed checkpoints

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Rely on the Project Manager for concise status, risk, dependency, and decision information
- Partner with the Product Manager on priority, value, and outcome trade-offs
- Support Engineering Managers and Developers when organizational or resource constraints block delivery
- Consider QA, Security / Compliance, Data / Analytics, and Customer Success / Support input before approving release or risk decisions

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Assign the relevant persona to project checkpoints, decisions, and artifacts so ownership and escalation paths are explicit.
